App name: Triathlon Tools
Subtitle: Calculators & utilities for training and racing

Description:
Triathlon Tools is a collection of calculators and utilities designed for triathletes and cyclists. Whether you're planning a race, dialing in your training zones, or setting up your bike, every tool you need is in one place.

Features:
• Pace Calculator — Calculate run, bike, and swim pace for any distance or target time
• Race Planner — Estimate your triathlon finish time with swim/bike/run split breakdowns
• Power Zones — Enter your FTP to see all 7 Coggan power training zones in watts
• IM Qualification — Calculate your age-graded finish time for Ironman World Championship qualification
• Climbing Calculator — Estimate climb time from power, or find the watts needed to hit a target time
• Tire Pressure — Calculate optimal front and rear tire pressure based on rider weight and tire width
• Gear Ratio — See speed for every chainring/cog combo with a color-coded heat map
• Stem Align — Use your camera to check if your bike stem and handlebar are perfectly centered
• Race Day Checklist — Track and customize your gear packing list with progress tracking
